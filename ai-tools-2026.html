15 AI tools worth exploring in 2026

A practical starting list across writing, research, design, video and productivity. Rather than claiming one universal winner, this guide explains what each tool is designed to help with and what to check before choosing.

By DigitalPickHub Editorial Team · Published 1 November 2026 · Research-led introductory guide

How to use this list

AI products change quickly. Features, regional availability, usage limits and prices can change without notice. Treat this as a discovery list, then confirm the current details on each provider’s official website before creating an account or paying. The tools below are examples, not a ranked or independently tested top 15.

Writing, research and everyday assistance

  1. ChatGPT: a general-purpose assistant for brainstorming, explaining topics, drafting, summarising and working through ideas. Check the current plan limits and verify important facts.
  2. Claude: an AI assistant that can help with writing, analysis and working with documents. Compare its output on your own tasks rather than relying on a single benchmark.
  3. Google Gemini: Google’s AI assistant for questions, writing, learning and creative tasks. Check which features are available in your country and account.
  4. Grammarly: writing support focused on clarity, correctness and tone, with AI assistance in supported products.
  5. Notion AI: AI features within Notion’s workspace for working with notes, documents and team knowledge.

Design, images and creative work

  1. Canva: a browser and mobile design platform with templates and AI-assisted creative features. Check the usage allowance attached to your plan.
  2. Adobe Firefly: Adobe’s generative creative tools for images and other visual content. Review the current terms and commercial-use conditions for the feature you use.
  3. Leonardo AI: a platform for generating and editing visual assets. Check generation limits, output rights and any watermark rules.

Video, audio and content production

  1. CapCut: a video editor with AI-assisted tools that can support short-form content workflows. Features may differ by device and region.
  2. Runway: a creative platform for AI-assisted video generation and editing. Review credits, export restrictions and commercial terms.
  3. Pika: a tool for creating and transforming short AI video clips. Check the live credit system before planning a project.
  4. Descript: an editing workflow built around audio and video transcripts, useful for interviews, podcasts and talking-head content.

Automation and work

  1. Zapier: connects supported apps and automates repetitive workflows. Check task limits and supported integrations.
  2. Make: a visual automation platform for connecting services and building multi-step workflows.
  3. Microsoft 365 Copilot: AI assistance integrated with Microsoft’s productivity ecosystem, subject to plan and account availability.

How to choose without wasting money

  • Start with the task you need to complete, not the trendiest product.
  • Try a free tier or trial only after checking whether it requires a payment card and what happens when the trial ends.
  • Test the same real task in two or three tools and compare the output, time saved and editing required.
  • Read privacy terms before uploading confidential, customer or personal information.
  • Check licensing, attribution, watermark and commercial-use rules before using generated work for a client or business.
