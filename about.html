ABOUT DIGITALPICKHUB

About Us

Last updated: 1 November 2026

DigitalPickHub is an independent digital discovery publication created to help people around the world make more informed choices about online tools and resources.

What we cover

Our editorial coverage focuses on AI tools, software and apps, online courses, digital marketing platforms, web services, hosting and website builders, and digital products such as templates and e-books.

Our purpose

The digital marketplace can be confusing. Products may have different plans, limitations, privacy practices and learning curves. Our goal is to make research easier by publishing practical explainers, beginner-friendly guides and comparisons that help readers identify what to check before choosing a service.

Our editorial standards

  • We aim to use clear, accessible language and explain who a tool may suit.
  • We distinguish publicly researched information from hands-on testing. We do not claim to have tested a product unless the article explicitly says so.
  • We encourage readers to verify current pricing, features, availability and terms on the provider's official website.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships and do not promise that a paid product is right for everyone.
  • We correct material errors when they are brought to our attention.

How the website is supported

Some links may be affiliate links. If a reader makes a qualifying purchase or signup through such a link, DigitalPickHub may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader in many programmes. This helps support the publication. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for details.
