Websites to learn digital skills online

A starter guide to learning design, marketing, technology and business skills through accessible online learning resources.

By DigitalPickHub Editorial Team · Published 1 November 2026 · Research-led introductory guide

Start with one skill and one goal

Online learning works best when you choose a specific outcome. Instead of trying to “learn tech” all at once, decide whether you want to design social media graphics, build websites, analyse data, run digital campaigns or write code. Then choose a course that includes practice, not just videos.

Free learning resources to explore

  • Google Skillshop: training on Google products and solutions, including topics such as advertising and analytics. Google says offered courses can be accessed at no cost.
  • HubSpot Academy: courses and certifications in areas such as inbound marketing, sales, content and customer service.
  • Microsoft Learn: self-paced technical learning paths and modules covering Microsoft technologies and broader technical skills.
  • freeCodeCamp: free, practice-oriented coding curriculum for people learning web development and programming.
  • Coursera: courses and professional learning programmes from universities and organisations. Some content may be free to audit while certificates and other features may require payment.
  • Nigeria Digital Training Academy: a Nigerian government-backed training initiative. Check the official site for current eligibility, application windows and available learning licences.

A simple 30-day learning approach

  1. Week 1: choose one skill, complete beginner lessons and write down key terms.
  2. Week 2: follow practical exercises and repeat them without copying every step.
  3. Week 3: create a small project, such as a sample poster, landing page, spreadsheet or campaign plan.
  4. Week 4: improve the project, ask for feedback and publish it in a simple portfolio.

How to avoid wasting money

Read the course outline before enrolling. Check whether lessons are current, whether assignments are included, whether a certificate costs extra and whether the course works on a phone. Do not pay for a certificate simply because an advert promises a job. A portfolio of completed work and the ability to explain what you built can be valuable alongside formal credentials.

For Nigerian learners: confirm that registration, payment and certificate access are available in Nigeria before starting a paid programme. If you only have a smartphone, choose mobile-friendly lessons and practical exercises that do not require a laptop at the beginning.
