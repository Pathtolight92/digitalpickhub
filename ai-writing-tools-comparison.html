AI writing tools: how to compare them for your work

A practical guide to choosing AI writing assistants for research, drafting, editing and everyday communication without trusting generated text blindly.

By DigitalPickHub Editorial Team · Published 1 November 2026 · Research-led introductory guide

There is no single best writing assistant

A tool that works well for brainstorming may not be the best choice for proofreading, long documents or writing inside an office suite. Choose based on the task, your budget, the languages you use and how much editing you are prepared to do.

Tools to put on your shortlist

  • ChatGPT: can assist with outlining, brainstorming, drafting, rewriting and explaining information. Verify factual claims and adapt the output to your voice.
  • Claude: an assistant to consider for drafting, editing and working through longer text or documents. Test it on your own writing samples.
  • Google Gemini: can help with writing and idea development, with features that may connect to supported Google services depending on account and plan.
  • Grammarly: focused on writing correctness, clarity and tone, with generative assistance in supported products.
  • QuillBot: offers writing utilities such as paraphrasing and summarising. Review the meaning carefully after rewriting.
  • Microsoft Copilot: an option to investigate if your work already uses Microsoft products. Availability depends on the product and subscription.

A quick test to compare tools

Choose a paragraph you wrote yourself and ask each shortlisted tool to improve clarity without changing the meaning. Compare whether it:

  • preserves your original facts and intended message;
  • uses a natural tone rather than generic phrases;
  • explains uncertain or missing information instead of inventing it;
  • handles your preferred language and spelling style;
  • lets you export or reuse the result in your normal workflow.

Use AI responsibly

AI-generated text can sound confident while being inaccurate. Check names, dates, quotations, statistics and references against reliable sources. Do not paste private customer information, passwords or confidential work into a service unless you understand its data controls and have permission. For school or professional work, follow the relevant rules on AI assistance and disclosure.
