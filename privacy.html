Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

DigitalPickHub ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit DigitalPickHub, how it may be used, and the choices available to you. Our website is an informational digital discovery publication for a worldwide audience and is operated from Nigeria.

1. Information we may process

Information you send us: If you email us, we receive the email address and any name, message or attachments you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Technical and usage information: Like most websites, our hosting and security provider (currently Cloudflare) may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps and security events in order to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the website. The exact logs retained and retention periods are controlled by the provider and applicable settings.

Cookies and similar technologies: DigitalPickHub does not currently operate its own account system or collect payment details. We do not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies ourselves at this time. Hosting, embedded content, or third-party links may use their own technologies. If we add analytics, advertising, embedded services or affiliate tracking that uses cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and provide any notices or consent controls required by applicable law.

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, respond to enquiries, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, maintain records of communications, understand website performance where permitted, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Affiliate links and third-party websites

Some pages may contain links to third-party products or services. If a link is an affiliate link, the provider or affiliate network may use cookies, referral identifiers or other tracking to attribute a purchase or signup. When you follow a third-party link, that provider's privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control third-party websites or their data practices. Review their policies before sharing information or making a purchase. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information for money. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host, secure or operate the website (including Cloudflare), by email providers when you contact us, by third-party sites you choose to visit, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

5. International visitors and data transfers

Because this website is available worldwide and uses online infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, service providers are expected to use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. Technical logs may be retained according to our hosting provider's settings and policies. We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small informational website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also adjust browser settings to manage cookies and may choose not to use affiliate or third-party links. To make a privacy request, email officialprimebusiness01@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to fulfil requests where an exception or legal obligation applies.

8. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

We may update this policy as the website, services or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact officialprimebusiness01@gmail.com.
