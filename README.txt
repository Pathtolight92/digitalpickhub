DIGITALPICKHUB STARTER WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: homepage
- styles.css: responsive design
- script.js: search, category filters and mobile navigation
- disclosure.html: affiliate disclosure draft
- privacy.html: privacy-policy placeholder (must be completed before launch)

HOW TO USE
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
3. Replace the placeholder content with researched product reviews and comparisons.
4. Apply to affiliate programmes that accept publishers in your country.
5. Only add your own approved affiliate URLs after acceptance.
6. Update contact details, privacy policy, disclosure and terms before publishing.

IMPORTANT
- This is a starter site, not a finished monetised business.
- Product cards currently contain no affiliate links and do not claim endorsement of specific products.
- Do not invent reviews, prices, ratings, results or affiliate relationships.
- Check each programme's country eligibility, traffic rules, disclosure requirements and payout methods.
- The example contact address hello@digitalpickhub.com is not configured; replace it with an email address you control.
- A custom domain is optional and is not included.
